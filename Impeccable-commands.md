# Commands

23 commands for design fluency. One skill, six categories, from shaping a new feature to hardening it for production.

## Create

Build something new, from a blank page to a working feature.

- [/impeccable craft](https://impeccable.style/docs/craft) — Shape the design, then build it, all in one flow.
- [/impeccable impeccable](https://impeccable.style/docs/impeccable) — The design intelligence behind every command.
- [/impeccable shape](https://impeccable.style/docs/shape) — Think before you build. Produce a design brief through discovery, not guesswork.

## Evaluate

Review what you have. Score it, critique it, find what to fix.

- [/impeccable audit](https://impeccable.style/docs/audit) — Five-dimension technical quality check with P0 to P3 severity.
- [/impeccable critique](https://impeccable.style/docs/critique) — A design review with scoring, persona tests, and automated detection.

## Refine

Improve one dimension at a time: type, layout, color, motion.

- [/impeccable animate](https://impeccable.style/docs/animate) — Purposeful motion that conveys state, not decoration.
- [/impeccable bolder](https://impeccable.style/docs/bolder) — Push safe designs toward impact without sliding into chaos.
- [/impeccable colorize](https://impeccable.style/docs/colorize) — Add strategic color to monochrome interfaces without going garish.
- [/impeccable delight](https://impeccable.style/docs/delight) — Small moments of personality that turn functional into memorable.
- [/impeccable layout](https://impeccable.style/docs/layout) — Fix layout, spacing, and visual rhythm.
- [/impeccable overdrive](https://impeccable.style/docs/overdrive) — Push an interface past conventional limits. Shaders, physics, 60fps, cinematic transitions.
- [/impeccable quieter](https://impeccable.style/docs/quieter) — Tone down designs that are shouting without losing their intent.
- [/impeccable typeset](https://impeccable.style/docs/typeset) — Fix typography that feels generic, inconsistent, or accidental.

## Simplify

Strip complexity. Remove what does not earn its place.

- [/impeccable adapt](https://impeccable.style/docs/adapt) — Make designs work across screens, devices, and contexts without amputating features.
- [/impeccable clarify](https://impeccable.style/docs/clarify) — Rewrite confusing UX copy so interfaces explain themselves.
- [/impeccable distill](https://impeccable.style/docs/distill) — Ruthless subtraction. Strip designs to their essence.

## Harden

Make it production-ready. Edge cases, performance, polish.

- [/impeccable harden](https://impeccable.style/docs/harden) — Make interfaces production-ready. Edge cases, i18n, error states, overflow.
- [/impeccable onboard](https://impeccable.style/docs/onboard) — Design first-run experiences, empty states, and paths to value.
- [/impeccable optimize](https://impeccable.style/docs/optimize) — Diagnose and fix UI performance from LCP to bundle size.
- [/impeccable polish](https://impeccable.style/docs/polish) — The meticulous final pass between good and great.

## System

Setup and tooling. Design system work, extraction, organization.

- [/impeccable document](https://impeccable.style/docs/document) — Generate a spec-compliant DESIGN.md that captures your visual system so every AI agent stays on-brand.
- [/impeccable extract](https://impeccable.style/docs/extract) — Pull reusable components, tokens, and patterns into the design system.
- [/impeccable live](https://impeccable.style/docs/live) — Iterate on UI in the browser. Pick an element, drop a comment, get three variants. Accept one and it writes to source.
- [/impeccable teach](https://impeccable.style/docs/teach) — Teach Impeccable who your product is for, once per project.
